  Read this honestly:

  * resolve() is the only figure on the worker's path. Everything else runs
    on the laptop and can be slow without anyone noticing.
  * The MB figure is the real ceiling. The whole approved record is held in
    memory on the phone so a scan needs no I/O and no radio — which is what
    makes the offline guarantee possible, and what puts a limit on project
    size per device. Past that limit the record would have to be scoped to
    the zones a worker actually covers, which is a change to the adapter and
    nothing else.
  * These are Node numbers on a laptop. A mid-range Android running Hermes is
    slower, and the honest multiplier is roughly 3-5x.
