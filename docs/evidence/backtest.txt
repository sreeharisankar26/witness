  The catch rate above is measured on this project's own record. The 5-15%

  three numbers in that chain is ours.

  WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEASURE

  * Sample size. 5 nonconformances is not a rate, it is an
    indication. A percentage from single figures should be read as "it caught
    the ones we have" and nothing stronger.
  * The record is synthetic. A real pilot would replay a contractor's own
    closed NCR log, which is the measurement that would actually settle this.
  * Whole classes of defect are invisible to it BY DESIGN. Witness compares an
    identity and a revision against an approved record. It cannot see bad
    workmanship, a part fitted the wrong way round, a correct revision
    installed in the wrong place by a worker who set the zone wrongly, or a
    part with no tag that nobody chose to scan. Those are not misses. They are
    outside what this system claims to do, and a version of it that claimed
    otherwise would be worse, not better.
  * It assumes the part was scanned. Coverage is a separate problem from
    accuracy, and the dashboard measures it separately for that reason.
