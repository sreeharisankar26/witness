Witness — Kaya AI · IIT India Hackathon 2026 · Track 1 (Physical AI)

Catches the wrong part before it goes into a building.

A worker points a phone at a part. In under a second, offline, they get a
spoken ruling: accepted, held, or refused. The thesis is one sentence — a model
reads the world, and never rules on it. Perception is a model; a compliance
ruling is a pure function over a local record, with no model anywhere on that
path, because on a building site a confidently wrong compliance ruling is a
safety incident.

Where to look first:

  app/src/engine/resolve.ts   the ruling. No model, no network, no clock.
  server/ingest.mjs           what is allowed to become "approved".
  server/ensemble.mjs         disagreement between two reads, as the signal.
  server/reorder.mjs          three wrong parts read as one bad delivery.
  docs/evidence/              benchmark, backtest and model-check output.
  docs/RED_TEAM_1.md          two adversarial reviews, and the bugs they found.

  cd app && npm test          189 tests, about two seconds, nothing to install.
